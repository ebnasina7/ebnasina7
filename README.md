## 🌟 Featured Project

<h3 align="center">⚽ FootScout: AI Football Scouting Engine</h3>

<p align="center"><i>"Find me a striker like Haaland under €80M", and it actually does.</i></p>

<p align="center">
  <a href="https://footscout.streamlit.app/"><img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/▶%20Try%20it%20live-footscout.streamlit.app-2C9FB8?style=for-the-badge&logo=streamlit&logoColor=white&labelColor=0f2027" alt="Live demo"/></a>
  <a href="https://github.com/sina-778/footscout"><img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Source-sina--778%2Ffootscout-203a43?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=white&labelColor=0f2027" alt="Code"/></a>
</p>

A player recommendation system built for my MSc at BHT. It works like Spotify's *Discover Weekly*, but for football scouts: type a question in plain English and get players who **play like** your target, with similarity scores and radar charts.

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

**What it does**
- 🔍 **AI Scout:** natural-language search (*"press-heavy striker like Firmino under €40M"*)
- 💶 **Budget Scout:** best match within your transfer budget
- 💎 **Hidden Gems:** strong stats, low market value
- 📊 Radar charts & real player photos

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

**How it works**
- 🧬 Each player is a **416-dim hybrid embedding**: 24 per-90 stats + Sentence-BERT text embeddings
- 📐 **Cosine similarity** finds who plays like whom
- 🎛️ Tuned with **Optuna**, tracked with **Weights & Biases**
- 🚀 Auto-deploys to **Streamlit Cloud** from GitHub

</td>
</tr>
</table>
