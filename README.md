## 🔭 What I'm Working On

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🗄️ NOMAD Oasis @ PDI
I run the institute's self-hosted **[NOMAD Oasis](https://nomad-lab.eu/)**, a platform where materials scientists store and share their research data.

- 🔐 Migrating the deployment from **HTTP to HTTPS**
- 📊 Building **custom dashboards** that pull data through the **NOMAD API**
- 🛠️ Keeping it reliable for the researchers who use it

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🧠 LoRA: Fine-tuning LLMs Efficiently
Group project in my Advanced ML course at BHT, based on **LoRA** (Hu et al., ICLR 2022).

- 💡 Instead of retraining a whole model, LoRA trains small **low-rank matrices**
- 📉 That cuts trainable parameters massively
- 🔬 We're studying and reproducing the paper's results

</td>
